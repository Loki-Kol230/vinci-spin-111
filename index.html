How the peg board works

The board is built as a triangle of staggered pegs. Every row gives the ball a left-or-right choice, so the path is a chain of small random turns. By the time it reaches the bottom, it will land in one of the pockets spread across the base.

That design creates a bell-shaped outcome pattern. Most balls end up near the middle, while the far left and far right pockets are less common. casino vincispin players often like this structure because it makes the game easy to read: central pockets appear more often, and the edge pockets carry the bigger multipliers.
